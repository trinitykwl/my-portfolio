About

Aerospace by training.
Builder by nature.

I am a final-year Aerospace Engineering student at Republic Polytechnic, drawn to aircraft structures, aerodynamics, and simulation-based design, with long-term aspirations in engineering R&D.

My projects span CubeSat payload systems for MangoSat (a 3U satellite built for multispectral Earth observation), a Pixhawk-based MRO inspection drone, and flight training on the Diamond DA-40 at SYFC. Whether it is orbital mission analysis in NASA GMAT, satellite downlink work in GNURadio, or designing the MangoSat mascot and BFC 289 course patch, I bring the same attention to detail to every layer of a project.

Outside engineering, I serve as Vice President of the IPSSC, the Institute of Physics Singapore Student Chapter, as the only pre-university representative in a national university-led body spanning NUS, NTU, and SUTD.
